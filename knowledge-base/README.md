# Knowledge Base — Claude's long-term memory 🧠

This folder holds **durable facts about your business and how you like to work**. Things that
are true today and likely still true next month.

## Why this exists

So you only have to tell Claude something **once**. Instead of re-explaining your business every
session, the important facts live here and stay available over time. This is what lets your
command center get *smarter the more you use it*.

## What to put here

- What your business does and who your customers are.
- Tools and services you use (and your logins lists — but **never** real passwords/secrets here).
- Decisions you've made and preferences ("I always want reports in bullet points").
- Important links, names, and recurring context.

## How to add to it

Just tell Claude: *"Remember this for the knowledge base: ..."* and it'll save it here.

## ⚠️ Safety note

**Never store passwords, API keys, or other secrets in these files.** This folder gets saved to
GitHub. If Claude ever needs a secret, it will tell you the safe way to provide it.
